Common questions

Before you download it

Is this Switchboard's own analysis, or independent research?

It's Switchboard's own perspective, based on what we see governing data for AI and analytics programs. We've written it to be useful on its own, whether or not you ever talk to us.

What's actually inside it?

Three things: why batch reporting creates a lag most teams underestimate, what has to be true of your data layer for real-time answers to actually work, and the questions to ask before you trust a “real-time” claim from any tool.

Does this apply if we're not on BigQuery yet?

The examples are BigQuery-first since that's where we see this most, but the underlying data governance problem doesn't change much by warehouse.

Who is this written for?

Revenue leaders at publishers and media companies: programmatic and yield, direct sales, ad ops, rev ops and BI. If you are not on the publisher side, it will be less directly useful.

Will someone from Switchboard reach out?

Only if it looks like a real fit. No call is required to read the playbook.
